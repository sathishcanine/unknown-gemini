Last Updated: July 28, 2026

Permanent & Irreversible

How to Delete Your Account

You can request deletion of your TNPSC Prep account and all associated data directly from within the app. No email or web form is required — deletion is instant and self-service.

  1. Open the TNPSC Prep app and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Tap the Profile icon/tab.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm by tapping Delete Permanently in the dialog.

Your account and data are deleted immediately upon confirmation — no waiting period.

What Data Is Deleted

When you delete your account, the following is permanently and immediately removed from our servers:

  • Your account record (Google email, sign-in identifier)
  • All test/quiz session history and results
  • All individual question answers you submitted
  • All activity logs associated with your account

What Data Is Retained

We do not retain any personal data after account deletion. No backups containing your personal data are kept beyond routine, short-lived database backup cycles, after which they are also purged.

Google Sign-In Access

Deleting your account within the app also revokes the app's local Google session on your device. If you'd like to fully revoke TNPSC Prep's access to your Google account from Google's side as well, you can do so anytime at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Need Help?

If you're unable to access the app to delete your account, contact us at suthishcanine@gmail.com with the email address associated with your account, and we will process the deletion request on your behalf within 7 days.
